**Configuration exposed to the app.** These addresses are served by
`GET /api/v1/config` and consumed by the frontend; they are never hardcoded, because a
network reset moves the Blend and DeFindex addresses
([`../testing.md`](../testing.md) §2). A token is only published to the app once every
address field is present — `contractAddress`, `issuer`, `vaquitaContractAddress`,
`defindexVaultContractAddress` and `blendPoolContractAddress` — enforced by
`packages/shared/src/services/project-config/readiness.ts` and its test suite.

**Lock periods live on mainnet:** 30 / 90 / 180 days.

**Yield source.** Deposits are routed into the DeFindex vault, which runs Blend lending
strategies. The mechanism — principal + own yield + pro-rata share of the forfeited-yield
reward pool at maturity, principal only on early exit — is specified in
[`../architecture.md`](../architecture.md) §1.

**Deployment procedure.** Mainnet deployment runs through manual `workflow_dispatch`
workflows against protected GitHub Environments with required reviewers, documented in
[`../mainnet-readiness-runbook.md`](../mainnet-readiness-runbook.md). Contract upgrades
follow the timelocked `propose_upgrade` → wait → `execute_upgrade` path in
[`../vaquita-pool-upgrade-runbook.md`](../vaquita-pool-upgrade-runbook.md).

### Composition of the six addresses

Two of the six are Vaquita's own Soroban contracts, built in this repository under
`contracts/`: `vaquita-pool` and `vaquita-badges`. The other four are the third-party and
asset contracts the deployment binds to — the DeFindex vault that runs the yield
strategies, the Blend pool behind it, the USDC token contract, and the Circle account that
issues USDC. Each was verified on Stellar Expert on 2026-08-26; all six are live and
carrying on-chain activity.

## 3. TVL on DeFiLlama

**Public listing:** https://defillama.com/protocol/vaquita-protocol

The protocol is listed as *Vaquita Protocol* (id `6475`), category *Yield Aggregator*, on
chains *Stellar* and *Base*, with a Stellar adapter reporting live TVL.

**Reported methodology** (DeFiLlama's own wording): on Stellar, TVL is the total assets
managed by the Vaquita-deployed DeFindex vault (`fetch_total_managed_funds`, covering both
idle and strategy-invested amounts), plus the idle deposit-token balance held by the
Vaquita pool contract to back the fixed-yield reward pool.

The figure on the page is live and moves with seed liquidity and user deposits; read it on
the page rather than from a number transcribed here.
