**Configuration exposed to the app.** These addresses are served by
`GET /api/v1/config` and consumed by the frontend; they are never hardcoded. A token is
only published to the app once every address field is present, enforced by
`packages/shared/src/services/project-config/readiness.ts` and its test suite.

**Lock periods live on mainnet:** 30 / 90 / 180 days.

**Yield source.** Deposits are routed into the DeFindex vault, which runs Blend lending
strategies. The mechanism — principal + own yield + pro-rata share of the forfeited-yield
reward pool at maturity, principal only on early exit — is specified in
[`../architecture.md`](../architecture.md) §1.

**Deployment procedure.** Mainnet deployment runs through manual `workflow_dispatch`
workflows against protected GitHub Environments with required reviewers, documented in
[`../mainnet-readiness-runbook.md`](../mainnet-readiness-runbook.md).

## 3. TVL on DeFiLlama

**Public listing:** https://defillama.com/protocol/vaquita-protocol

The protocol is listed as *Vaquita Protocol* (id `6475`), category *Yield Aggregator*, on
chains *Stellar* and *Base*, with a Stellar adapter reporting live TVL.

**Reported methodology** (DeFiLlama's own wording): on Stellar, TVL is the total assets
managed by the Vaquita-deployed DeFindex vault (`fetch_total_managed_funds`, covering both
idle and strategy-invested amounts), plus the idle deposit-token balance held by the
Vaquita pool contract to back the fixed-yield reward pool.

The figure on the page is live and moves with seed liquidity and user deposits.
